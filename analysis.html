Every number here is a typical week: ESPN’s full-season projection divided by 17 games. Nobody’s week is in this table — the grid below is. The nine columns are the best lineup that squad could field, chosen by that average rather than by where the manager has parked people; FLEX is the best remaining RB, WR or TE, never a QB. Total is those nine added up — nine real men, not an estimate. B1… are the bench, best first, with each man’s position in the cell because every bench is a different shape — and after it where he ranks at that position on his own team, counting the starters too, so RB4 is his squad’s fourth-best back whether or not the other three are in the lineup.
Hovering any number gives that man’s name and his whole season as a chart: the weeks along the top, his projection for each one underneath. Every name is in full in the roster detail below.
