Each starter is shown with his season average — ESPN’s full-season projection divided by 17 games — so these are typical weeks, not this week’s forecast. FLEX is the best remaining RB, WR or TE by that average; never a QB. Baseline week is those seven added up: what the lineup scores if everyone hits his average. Est Total = Baseline week + 16, a flat allowance for the D/ST and kicker at about 8 each. They are left out of the grid because they are consistent and get streamed constantly. The last two columns are this week specifically: the whole nine-man lineup’s projection and what it has actually scored so far, D/ST and kicker included.
