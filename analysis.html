How this grid works

Every number is a typical week: ESPN’s full-season projection divided by 17 games. Nobody’s week is in this table — the grid below is.

The nine columns are the best lineup that squad could field, chosen by that average rather than by where the manager has parked people. FLEX is the best remaining RB, WR or TE, never a QB. Total is those nine added up — nine real men, not an estimate.

B1… are the bench, best first. Each man’s position is in the cell because every bench is a different shape — and after it where he ranks at that position on his own team, counting the starters too, so RB4 is his squad’s fourth-best back whether or not the other three are in the lineup.

Tap or hover any number for that man’s name and his whole season as a chart: the weeks along the top, his projection for each one underneath. On a phone it opens as a panel at the foot of the screen, with a button through to his next 13 weeks. Every name is in full in the roster detail below.

Every number is a link to that man’s next 13 weeks on the Players page. Click the row anywhere else to load that team below, and click any header to sort.

How this grid works

The same table again, measured on ESPN’s projection for the week selected at the top of the page instead of on the season average — so read the two straight down to see who is better this week than they usually are.

The lineup is re-picked on that week’s numbers, so a squad’s FLEX can be a different man here than it is above. The bench ranks mean the same thing as above, measured on this week instead of the average.

A cell reading Bye is the 0.00 ESPN returns for a player whose NFL team is off that week, which is not the same as having no number at all. ESPN also returns 0.00 for a man it has ruled out, so a zero outside his team’s bye week reads 0.0 with OUT, IR or SUSP beside it.

Tapping or hovering a number gives that man’s whole season as a chart — weeks along the top, his projection for each underneath — and every number is a link to his next 13 weeks on the Players page, both the same as in the grid above.
