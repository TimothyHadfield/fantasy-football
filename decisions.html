How this works

A move is undone and the season replayed: every later add, drop and trade happens again in order, if it still can.

Other teams keep their real lineups. A slot a missing player leaves takes their best-projected bench player.

Reasonable starts whoever ESPN projected highest, every week; Perfect hindsight whoever scored most. All users does it for every team at once.

Noise is how unsure a number is: other managers would have reacted. It grows each week after the decision, and where a freed player could have been picked up. Dimmer is less sure.
