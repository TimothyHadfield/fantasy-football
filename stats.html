Cumulative luck

Luck for the season so far, after each week. Above zero means the season has broken your way.

How this works

Each point is computed from every week up to that one, not from that week alone, so the lines converge as the season goes on and single results stop dominating.

How to read it

Each row counts every game whose projected margin was bigger than the row’s figure, so the rows overlap. Bigger margins should predict better.

Score spread by team

How much each team’s score moves week to week. The box is its middle half of weeks; the line is its median.

How to read it

Dots are outlier weeks beyond 1.5× the interquartile range (the width of the box).
